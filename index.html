About

Jost Neumann is based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and coordinates the Tourism for Rural Development Programme of UN Tourism, the United Nations specialized agency for tourism. The role covers advocacy, advisory services and technical and financial assistance for Member States, the coordination of the Best Tourism Villages by UN Tourism (BTV) Network and the Tourism for Rural Development Small Grants Programme.

From 2018 to 2025, Jost Neumann held programme leadership roles at the TUI Care Foundation. As Director Programmes (2022–2025), Jost Neumann oversaw the investment of approximately EUR 40 million of philanthropic capital in destination projects on entrepreneurship, education and nature conservation and the mobilization of more than EUR 12 million from institutional donors. Several projects received awards, including the Responsible Tourism Award and the German Sustainability Award.

Earlier positions include economic recovery and communications roles at the International Rescue Committee, responsible investment in Eastern and Southern Africa, and strategy work at the Vodafone Institute. The work combines expertise in philanthropy, impact investing and innovative finance with field experience in South-East Asia, Africa and the Middle East.

Lab

Small tools and experiments built with Claude, an AI model by Anthropic. They are personal projects: the ideas, design decisions, testing and final checks are Jost Neumann's own.

  • Prototype · Open data

    BTV Digital Explorer

    An open data explorer of the 236 Best Tourism Villages by UN Tourism (BTVs) in 57 countries. It brings together open data frameworks maintained largely by international organizations: settlement type and population from the Global Human Settlement Layer, access to towns, airports and railway stations, international recognitions such as UNESCO sites and protected areas, and documented good practices graded by the strength of their evidence. Its maps are designed to follow United Nations cartographic conventions, and it is available in English, French, Spanish and Arabic.

    Internal prototype, not public · Built with Claude Code

  • Tool · Web app

    Samer, a speaking companion

    Turns a Word or PDF speech into a teleprompter in the browser. Sections and key messages are found automatically and stay editable, cues for the speaker are not read aloud, the pace follows the time slot, and a speech can be sent to a phone by link or QR code. It detects Arabic and works offline. The name means a companion in evening conversation in Arabic.

    Private preview · Built with Claude Code · Code on GitHub

  • Website · Pixel art

    This room

    The scene is drawn in code, pixel by pixel. Light and seasons follow the visitor's local time and date, wildlife and weather appear now and then, a framed print draws one-line homages to famous works, and a generated soundscape of rain and nature plays on request. No images, fonts or scripts are loaded from third parties.

    Built with Claude Code · October 2026
